## Source inventory and preparation

| Source ID / type | Original location, revision and cut-off | Unit/assets and coverage/exclusions | Authority by fact / responsible owner | Fields/units/identity mapping and preparation needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Not recorded | Not recorded | Unknown | Needs owner | Unknown |

Preserve exact source locators and hashes when producing a toolkit packet. An export date is not necessarily
an engineering release date. Record missing exports and unknown coverage rather than treating absence as proof.
For each physical replacement retain position/tag, old/new model/serial, observed date and evidence; a matching
tag alone does not identify which physical unit was observed.

## Expected receiving decision

Record what must be demonstrated: another person can reconstruct the answer; each material disagreement has
a responsible disposition; the recipient verifies the accepted ID/value/revision in the existing system; earlier
evidence survives; one subsequent change is handled. List unresolved exclusions and who can accept them.
This form is a scope record, not permission for field work, change, startup or publication.
