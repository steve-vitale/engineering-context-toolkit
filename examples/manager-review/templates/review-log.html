Use the company's status scheme. A simple manual sequence is needs owner → evidence needed → review requested → correction requested → receiving verified → closed. Record no-change dispositions and reopen history too. These words are not implemented toolkit states or a workflow engine.

Before closing an action, retain the reviewed basis and residual limits, the authoritative receiving ID/value/ revision, who verified it and when, and the link to that company record. Acknowledging a message or resolving a local disagreement does not demonstrate that the source record was corrected. If the receiving check fails, keep the action open with its next owner.

If a source changes, retain the previous entry and create a dated review of its affected basis. Deduplicate repeated reports of the same condition without hiding a new revision. Record withdrawn access, missing exports and unresolved owner changes explicitly. Row rank, software color and amount of text are not an approved maintenance priority, incident severity or complete physical impact assessment.
