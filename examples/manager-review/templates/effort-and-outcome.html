Sum all included person-hours separately for each method; retain elapsed delay separately. For a valid matched comparison, hours difference = manual total − trial total. Do not omit setup, review or correction effort. Missing activities make the comparison incomplete. A negative result is retained rather than presented as a saving.

Record incorrect/unsupported conclusions, false asset matches, missed affected records, corrections and residual unknowns using the same definition and denominator for each method. Counts of known errors are not an accuracy rate unless the reference and eligible population have been independently reviewed.
