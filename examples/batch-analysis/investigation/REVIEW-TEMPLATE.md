# One investigation handoff — manual template

- Question and exact subject/batch namespace:
- Observation: quantity, value, unit, sample/observation time, availability cutoff, exact source revision,
  record ID and CSV/JSON locator; state calculation, quality, mapping and clock assumptions:
- Authored hypothesis and its limits (not an observation):
- Planned record check; supporting condition, falsifier, missing/uninterpretable condition; not a process trial:
- Actual check result, or explicitly not performed/unresolved; selected manifest/report/code and dated check output:
- Supporting and contradicting evidence, including late/unavailable evidence kept outside the decision view:
- Proposed disposition, separate from the observed outcome; no authority inferred:
- Unresolved alternatives and next required records (lab/recipe/execution/model), with an unassigned receiving role:
- Revision dependencies: every relied-on report/source, exact prior/current pins and selected revisions:
- Where originals, failed observations, check outputs, prior hypothesis/disposition and new revision are retained:

Keep this in the existing approved company evidence/decision home. Identify its owner and access rules;
do not put company records in a public example. Generic knowledge capture does not validate these roles.
